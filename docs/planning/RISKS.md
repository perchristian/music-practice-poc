# Risks

## Domain Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Current status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Poor stem separation quality from compressed, noisy, or densely mixed sources | Medium | High | Keep mock mode demoable; test real recordings early; compare multiple separators only after first real spike | Partially mitigated. Demucs `htdemucs_6s` was accepted as good enough for the `MakeYouFeelMyLovePart2.mov` play-along workflow, but more songs are still needed |
| Any instrument leaks across stems, in any direction | Medium | High | Inspect separated stems manually; every stem is now both removed and studied, so leakage matters symmetrically | Still open. Demucs improves the accompaniment use case, but crackle/artifacts and leakage remain possible in any stem |
| A stem is too poor after separation to be studied in isolation | Medium | High | Evaluate every stem by soloing it, not only by removing it; let users import stems separated elsewhere when pipeline quality is insufficient | Open, and broadened on 2026-08-08. Piano removal was good enough for POC play-along on `MakeYouFeelMyLovePart2.mov`, and drums isolation was rated "not perfect but ok" by the product owner. Bass, guitar, vocals, and other are unmeasured. Decision 19's rationale no longer holds; stem import is the structural mitigation |
| The user's source material is not one they lawfully hold, or the app is framed as a capture tool for streaming services | Medium | High | Keep the app local and single-user; document that the user supplies material they already have; do not build or market streaming acquisition; prioritize stem import as the cleanest input path | Partially mitigated on 2026-08-08 by the reframe pass. `docs/research/source-legality-and-legal-posture.md` records the position and the open questions for counsel |
| Beat/bar grid is wrong or drifts because Bar 1, tempo spans, or mid-song meter changes are misidentified, making chord placement, loops, count-in, and practice notes misleading | Medium | High | Analyze timing before harmony; keep the grid audible and analyzer provenance immutable; use one bar-line editor for downbeat time and effective meter; derive tempo sparsely between bar events; use a threshold-aware timing benchmark that rejects ordinary beat jitter while detecting persistent phase/tempo changes | Partially mitigated. Version-2 timing events represent downbeat and meter changes through one bar line, and seven generated fixtures pass the locked sparse-timing gate with at most 60 ms bar error. Human review passed changing-tempo and late-start recordings for corrected click, loops, count-in, reload, and chord highlighting. Loop-entry, snapshot remapping, and bar/beat-authoritative boundaries are automated and human-confirmed. Broader song and device coverage remains open |
| Chord detection is wrong or misleading because melody, ornaments, missing bass, separation leakage, or independent repeated-section estimates dominate stable accompaniment harmony | High | High | Treat chord reliability as a pre-feature validation gate; benchmark reference-timed and end-to-end analysis separately on a locked split; preserve user-owned charts; after a failed gate, keep the failure immutable and allow only explicitly bounded follow-ups | Confirmed core blocker. Chordino materially beat the local analyzer but failed the frozen root gate; its manual review then failed on `ShapeOfMyHeart`. CR2F Candidate A passed development with only one fewer false-extra boundary and three fewer local `ShapeOfMyHeart` cues; issue #12 must determine whether wrong roots remain before Candidate B or STOP. Product integration remains blocked. |
| Returning to analyzer suggestions destroys a user's chord work | Low | High | Require explicit confirmation; snapshot the exact chart against the active analysis identity; permit one-step undo only before newer edits; never merge or train on user work | Mitigated for the POC. Both ordinary reset and corrected-timing replacement preserve an analysis-scoped backup, stale identities are rejected, and automated backend/browser coverage verifies exact restore |
| Benchmark results overfit one corpus or fail to represent screen-recording audio | High | High | Stratify RWC-P by chord and instrumentation complexity; use each fixed holdout once; use synthetic/solo-instrument corpora only for diagnostics; treat the fixed local screen recordings as development evidence after review; require fresh evidence before any replacement claim | Confirmed uncertainty. The CR2E holdout and all three manual scenarios are consumed. CR2F may tune only on the RWC development split and those local recordings; even a pass authorizes fresh validation planning, not product integration or a general accuracy claim. |
| CPU-only processing is too slow for a convincing demo | Medium | High | Keep mock mode default; measure first real subsystem before committing to full real pipeline | Still open for user-length recordings. Demucs completed the 23-second bakeoff clip and a short end-to-end smoke locally, but longer screen recordings need measurement |
| Full-song harmonic analysis exhausts memory or becomes too slow | Medium | High | Analyze a reduced-rate mono representation, retain a bounded upload guard, and regression-test cues beyond two minutes | Partially mitigated. The 120-second truncation is removed and a 125-second fixture completes with cues after 120 seconds; the upload guard is now 650 MB to accommodate five-minute screen recordings, while runtime and peak memory still need measurement on several real user-length recordings with all supporting stems |
| iOS Photos permissions, large video uploads, and App Transport Security create friction | Medium | Medium | Start with web/local uploader; revisit native only if upload friction blocks validation | Open |
| Copyright and user expectations around commercial recordings | High | Medium | Document demo media limitations; avoid bundling commercial media | Open |

## Dependency and Platform Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Current status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Heavy ML dependencies slow or fail installation | High | High | Keep default mock mode dependency-light; add real dependencies only through explicit setup | Partially mitigated locally with `.venv-real`, `requirements-real.txt`, and `.cache/torch`; still a setup risk on new machines |
| Chordino replacement adds Sonic Annotator/Vamp packaging and copyleft review | Medium | High | Keep Chordino and NNLS Chroma benchmark-only through CR2F; pin transforms; install tools separately; require a separate product-owner decision before adapter work | Deferred. Universal ARM/Intel Sonic Annotator 1.7 and Vamp Plugin Pack 2.0 work locally, but product distribution, subprocess peak memory, failure handling, and GPL/AGPL obligations remain unassessed. A CR2F pass would only make that decision ready. |
| FFmpeg unavailable in development environment | Medium | Medium | Do not require FFmpeg for mock mode; document real-mode setup; return a clear missing-FFmpeg error in real mode | Mitigated locally on 2026-07-06 by Homebrew install; verified `/opt/homebrew/bin/ffmpeg` version 8.1.2. Automated missing-FFmpeg coverage now verifies the failure path |
| Browser audio behavior differs across desktop and iOS Safari | Medium | Medium | Use native HTML audio APIs first; test on target devices before user sessions | Open |
| Multiple HTML audio elements drift during synchronized stem playback | Medium | Medium | Keep sync simple in mock mode; measure drift manually; move to Web Audio if drift distracts from the demo | Open |
| Large uploads consume local disk or memory | High | High | Keep a 650 MB hard cap; run the local backend on a machine with sufficient free memory and disk; replace buffered upload storage with streaming before broader real user testing | Open for five-minute recordings. The cap now accommodates the observed screen-recording bitrate, but the native multipart decoder and WAV reader still buffer source data in memory, and peak memory/disk usage has not been measured with a near-limit upload |
| GUI verification depends on Playwright browser installation | Medium | Low | Keep Playwright dev-only; document `npx playwright install chromium`; backend tests still run without browser binaries | Mitigated |

## Sharing and Portability Risks

New with Decisions 43-49. None of this is implemented; the rows record what the
specifications are designed against.

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Current status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Extracting engines and controls into shared packages regresses working, human-verified behavior | Medium | High | Extract in dependency order, one subsystem at a time, moving algorithms rather than rewriting them; start with the already-pure, already-tested modules | Open; sequence in `docs/engineering/PORTABILITY.md` |
| A WebView-hosted interface cannot schedule audio accurately enough on iOS | Medium | High | Keep audio behind an `AudioEngine` provider so playback can drop to native AVAudioEngine without touching the UI; verify on real hardware before claiming the capability | Open, unmeasured |
| Cloud sync creates conflicted-copy files the app cannot interpret | Low | Medium | Band folder mode is append-only: media written once per song, every chart correction a new uniquely named file, so two members never write the same path | Open; specified, unimplemented |
| Band folder mode works on desktop but not on iPad, leaving iOS members out of step | Medium | Medium | iOS gets an explicit manual check rather than a promise of background watching; verify on a real device; a self-hosted sync server remains the escape hatch | Open, unmeasured |
| An imported bundle is malicious or malformed and damages the local library | Low | High | Treat bundles as untrusted archives: reject traversal, symlinks, oversized and over-compressed entries; validate against a schema before applying anything; never execute anything from a bundle | Open; posture specified |
| Two members correct the same chart at once and one loses an evening's work | Low | High | One owner per song, so only the owner's edits are published; ownership changes rarely, so it meets the sync race window rarely; the append-only log keeps both sets of work if a takeover is raced | Open; specified |
| Ownership is mistaken for enforced permission | Medium | Medium | Present it as coordination, not protection; there is no authentication behind it; never prevent a member editing their own copy; attribute every takeover | Open, a wording and interaction risk |
| A schema change breaks songs a musician has already corrected | Medium | High | Version every persisted and exported structure, ship a forward migration with a committed fixture, and preserve unknown fields on round trip | Open, applies from the first released format |
| Charts bind to the wrong copy of a recording after a chart-only import | Medium | High | Media enclosed by default removes the binding step entirely; chart-only binds automatically only on exact fingerprint match and requires confirmation on a duration-only match | Open; specified |
| Specifying three capabilities before the chord gate closes invites scope creep | Medium | High | BR3-BR5 are specified but explicitly not scheduled, and do not preempt CR2F/MR0; each names its non-goals | Open; the main process risk of this batch |
